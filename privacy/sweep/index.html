Privacy Policy

Sweep: No-Guess Minesweeper · Last updated 31 August 2026

Short version: nothing leaves your device. Sweep has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no network access at all. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data — or any data of any kind.

1. Who we are

Sweep: No-Guess Minesweeper is published by GadGames. Contact: support@tug.today.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. There is no way to create an account, no advertising identifier is requested, and the app makes no network requests. On Android the app does not even hold the internet permission, which your device can verify.

The app stores the following only on your device, using the operating system's normal app storage: your board in progress, statistics and streaks, achievements, and your settings. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and we cannot see any of it.

3. Purchases and advertising

There are no purchases of any kind in the app, and no advertising: no ads are shown, no advertising SDK is included, and nothing about you is shared with any advertising network.

If that ever changes, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released.

4. Analytics and third parties

The app contains no analytics service, no crash-reporting service and no third-party SDK that processes data. Since the app never connects to the internet, no server — ours or anyone else's — ever hears from it.

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Everything you play — progress, statistics, streaks, achievements and settings — is held only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no account to close and no copy anywhere else. The app also offers "Reset all progress" in Settings if you want a clean slate without reinstalling.

7. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you: no account, no contact details, and no identifier that points to you. There is therefore nothing for us to produce or erase. If you would like this confirmed in writing, email us and we will reply.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what the app does will be published before the version that makes the change is released.

Privacy Policy

Quilted · Shikaku Rectangles · Last updated 24 September 2026

Short version: nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Your puzzles, your quilt book, your streak and your coins live on your device. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (which levels are played, where boards are left unfinished) that carry no name, no account and no advertising identifier, and you can switch them off in Settings.

1. Who we are

Quilted · Shikaku Rectangles is published by GadGames. Contact: support@tug.today.

2. Information we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, there is no way to create an account, and no advertising identifier is requested.

Anonymous usage statistics. To learn which difficulties are too hard or too easy, the app records a few game events: the app was opened, a board was started, solved or left unfinished (with its level, the time spent and how much was filled in), a hint or check was used (and which technique it showed), the Sewing Box was opened, a purchase or restore completed (the product, never payment details), and a "More games" card was tapped. Each event carries a random identifier created on your device the first time the app runs. It is not derived from your device, your Apple ID or anything else, it is never combined with other data, and deleting the app deletes it. Events are sent to PostHog (posthog.com), the analytics service we use, which stores them for us; they are not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage, and nothing further is sent.

The app stores the following only on your device, using the operating system's normal app storage: the board you are working on, the daily blocks you have sewn, your statistics and streak, your coin balance and your settings. We cannot see any of it.

The app never asks you for a permission. It does not use the camera, the photo library, the microphone, contacts or location. Apart from the App Store and the anonymous statistics above, it contacts no server.

3. Purchases

Coins are a consumable purchase, bought in packs and spent on help during a puzzle (a Hint or a Check). Unlimited Help is a one-time non-consumable unlock. You can also earn a coin without paying, by finishing the daily block. No puzzle is locked behind a purchase, and there is no subscription.

Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. The store tells the app whether a purchase completed so that it can credit what you paid for, and reports sales to us only as aggregate figures that identify nobody.

Restoring a purchase asks the store whether your account owns the Unlimited Help unlock. That exchange is between your device and the store; no account with us is created or needed. Coins, being consumable, cannot be restored by any app.

4. Advertising

The app contains no advertising. There is no advertising SDK in it, no advertising identifier is requested, and nothing about you is shared with any advertising network or data broker.

If that ever changes — the only format we would ever add is a short video you choose to watch in exchange for a coin — this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released, and the App Store privacy label will be updated with it.

5. Sharing a result

After a daily block you can tap Share. The app hands a short line of text (the block number, your time and whether you used hints) to your phone's own share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose where it goes.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. It does not collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Your games are held only on your device, so deleting the app deletes them. There is no account to close. Anonymous statistics carry no identifier linked to you, so we cannot single out yours; switching them off stops any more being sent.

8. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold no data that identifies you, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. If you would like this confirmed in writing, email us and we will reply.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what the app does will be published before the version that makes the change is released.

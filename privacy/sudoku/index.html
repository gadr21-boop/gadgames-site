Privacy Policy

Sudoku — Classic Number Game · Last updated 20 August 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no server of its own. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Sudoku — Classic Number Game is published by Gad Roash (GadGames). Contact: support@tug.today.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and there is no way to create an account. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app.

The app stores the following only on your device, using the operating system's normal app storage: puzzle progress, completion statistics and streaks, your settings, and your coin balance. We cannot see any of it.

3. Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a purchase was completed, so that it can unlock what you paid for, and reports sales to us only as aggregate figures that identify nobody.

Restoring a purchase asks Apple whether your Apple ID owns it. That exchange is between your device and Apple; no account with us is created or needed.

4. Advertising

The app contains no advertising. There is no advertising SDK in it, no advertising identifier is requested, and nothing about you is shared with any advertising network.

If that ever changes, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released.

5. Analytics and tracking

There is no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about how you play leaves your device.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. Because it collects nothing at all, there is nothing about a child to collect or disclose.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Because the data never leaves your device, deleting the app deletes it. There is no account to close, no request to submit, and no copy held anywhere else.

8. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or erase. If you would like this confirmed in writing, email us and we will reply.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what the app does will be published before the version that makes the change is released.

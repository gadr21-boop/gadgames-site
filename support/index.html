I bought Unlimited Hints and it is gone

Open the game, go to the Store, and tap Restore purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to the app, so it comes back without any sign-up, password, or email. This is what to do after reinstalling the app or moving to a new device.

It will not restore if you are signed in with a different Apple ID than the one you bought with, or if you have moved between iPhone and Android — those are separate stores with separate purchases.

I reinstalled and my coins are gone

Coins are a consumable purchase, and consumables cannot be restored by any app — that is how the App Store works, not a choice we made. Unlimited Hints is a permanent unlock precisely because it does survive a reinstall.

How do I earn coins without paying?

You start with 10, and solving the daily challenge earns one more every day. Coins are only ever spent on help — every puzzle in the game can be solved by thinking, with no coins at all.

Are there ads?

No interruptions, no banners, and no ads between puzzles. Nothing takes over your screen and nothing stops the audio you are listening to.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything the game stores — your progress, statistics, settings and coin balance — lives only on your device, and goes with it. There is no account to close and no server holding a copy.
